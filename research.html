Research

Here is some of my research, with work spanning auditory perception in mixed reality, audio interfaces for extended reality, and new forms of musical interaction.

Experiences

  1. Voc-Synth: A new voice-controlled instrument concept

    2025–

    I proposed replacing the keyboard control of a physical synthesizer instrument with voice control, collaborating with Electrical Engineering students to prototype the design and brainstorming methods of new intuitive instrument controls for the hands.

  2. Designing Audiovisual Widgets for Extended Reality

    2025–

    Augmented Perception Lab · Human-Computer Interaction Institute

    Advisors: Hyunsung Cho, David Lindlbauer

    I spearheaded the design of novel audiovisual widgets within Extended Reality (XR) environments. I analyzed seminal literature on auditory cues and icons in order to come up with the next iteration for XR, and prototyped a new prompt-controlled synthesizer and audio generator to integrate with XR interfaces.

  3. Spatial Audio and the Ventriloquist Effect in VR

    2026–

    Auditory Lab · Department of Psychology

    Advisor: Laurie Heller

    I designed and executed experiments to measure spatial audio localization thresholds of the ventriloquist effect in virtual reality, developing VR environments and auditory stimuli scripts in Unity to manipulate visual and spatial cues. I analyzed user localization data to understand multisensory perception, and am co-authoring an upcoming November 2026 conference poster/abstract.

  4. Personal Soundtrack Project

    2025–

    Augmented Perception Lab · Human-Computer Interaction Institute

    Advisors: Alexander Wang, David Lindlbauer

    I investigated adaptive soundtrack systems and personal music paradigms, implementing prototypes to study music adaptation to user context.
