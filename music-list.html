Lunar Gala 2025

Immensity

Designers: Erika Egliskis, Sunny Guo

Composed for Carnegie Mellon's Lunar Gala fashion show. The designers were aiming for an East Asian ocean theme; I sought to give the viewer an underwater experience to match the aquatic outfits, combining orchestral instrumentation and synths.
